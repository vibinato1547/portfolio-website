Event videography

Looking for a videographer for your gigs and events? Having promoted music events myself I know what the demands are, and realise that everyone has different needs and budgets. This is why I offer three different packages, all including filming, editing, colour grading and after-effects.

Tier 1: Instagram reels package. 3 reels to match the vibe of your social media posts. Includes editing, colour grading and after effects.

Tier 2: Highlights package: If you'd like to showcase your event on Youtube, I can make a stylish 5 minute video of your event. Ideal for start-up club nights and DJ sets. Also includes optimisation for Instagram. Here is an example.

Tier 3: The full event. A full concert film of your event. Recommended for concerts and larger events/festivals.

For more information and rates feel free to enquire to ernest.nabialek@proton.me. Don't have the budget for a videographer but have a cool event you'd like to talk about? Get in touch and we can take it from here.
